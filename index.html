Our Story

Born on the Silk Road

We are a team of passionate Georgian guides, storytellers, and adventure lovers who believe that travel is the most powerful way to connect cultures. Since 2012, we've been crafting authentic journeys that go beyond the postcard — into the kitchens, monasteries, and mountain trails that make Georgia unlike anywhere else on earth.

Every itinerary is built around genuine human encounters: a winemaker sharing his family's ancient recipe, a shepherd whose flock has grazed these hills for generations, a chef who transforms simple ingredients into a feast.
